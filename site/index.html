A whole week,
planned in one tap.

Sabji, dal, rotli and bhaat every day, the way your house actually eats. The planner keeps it balanced without anyone thinking about it.

  • No sabji twice in the same week, and never the same vegetable two days in a row.
  • Undhiyu waits for Sunday. Long, fancy dishes land on weekends, quick ones on busy weekdays.
  • Pav bhaji once, not daily. Outside food and chatpata dishes are capped every week.
  • Ekadashi sorted. Mark a fast day and only farali food shows up.

Measured for your family,
not a cookbook's.

Cooking for 4 tonight and 9 on Sunday? Every quantity changes with it. Salt and masala grow slower than the dal, the way any mom adjusts by hand.

Balanced plate,
without counting.

Calories, protein, fibre, sugar and salt for every serving. When aloo sabji and rotli fall short on protein, it suggests a katori of curd or sprouts on the side.
